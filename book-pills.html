The Life Between Two Pills

One final day. Two pills. A truth hidden behind silence.

Julian is twenty-one, and time is running out. As he faces what may be his final day, a mysterious arrangement with his doctor leaves questions no one seems willing to answer.

Between family, old memories, and someone who has always been closer than words could explain, Julian tries to make the most of the time he has left.

But some truths are harder to swallow than any pill.
