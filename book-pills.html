The Life Between Two Pills

By Midnight Scribbler
Genre: Drama • Mystery

About the Book

Julian is twenty-one, and time is running out. He has only one day left, yet he wants to spend it as though nothing has changed.

Behind a mysterious arrangement involving two pills and his doctor lies a truth Julian is trying to keep hidden from the people closest to him.

Between his family, old memories, and Elena, someone who has been part of his life for years, Julian tries to make the most of the time he has left.

But as the day unfolds, silence begins to reveal more than words ever could.
