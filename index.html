The Story

The Memory We Lost is a fast-paced psychological thriller centered on Ethan Carter, a man forced to confront a past he has no memory of surviving. The night begins at 2:17 a.m. when Ethan receives a cryptic call and unsettling text messages from an unknown sender warning him not to return to an old, isolated estate known as Blackwood. Along with the warning comes a photograph of his own car parked outside the mysterious house — and wet footprints left inside his apartment.

Driven to uncover the truth, Ethan teams up with his neighbor Daniel and a woman from his past, Claire. Together, they are drawn back to Blackwood and a hidden basement facility known as "Room 17". As they navigate a trail of cassette tapes, classified documents, and encrypted files linked to a covert initiative called "Project 17", Ethan discovers that his memories were deliberately altered and that every answer leads back to October 14, 2012.
