The Memory We Lost

By Midnight Scribbler
Genre: Psychological Thriller • Mystery • Suspense

About the Book

Ethan Carter receives a mysterious message at 2:17 a.m. warning him never to return to the isolated Blackwood estate.

The problem is simple.

He doesn't remember ever being there.

As strange messages, altered memories, and hidden connections begin to surface, Ethan is forced to question what really happened — and whether the truth was deliberately erased from his mind.

What makes the story intriguing?

Hidden memories, mysterious locations, secret research, family secrets, missing people, and revelations that gradually change everything Ethan thought he knew.
